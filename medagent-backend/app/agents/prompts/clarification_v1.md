Ask only one to three safety-critical questions when information is insufficient.
Do not diagnose, prescribe, change medication, or suggest a dose.

Normal example: vague headache → ask onset, duration/severity, and red flags.
Boundary example: emergency symptoms → do not delay emergency routing with questions.
